## 📋 EXAM PATTERN SUMMARY
- **Duration**: 3 Hours | **Max Marks**: 100 | **Weightage**: 70%
- **Q1**: Compulsory (8 sub-questions × 5 marks = 40 marks)
- **Q2-Q5**: Attempt any 3 (each 20 marks, usually 2×10 or 1×10+2×5 or 4×5)
- **Short Notes**: 4×5=20 marks (appears frequently in Q5)

## 📌 KEY INSIGHTS & STRATEGY

### Exam Pattern Observations:
1. **Q1 is 40 marks** - Always 8 sub-questions of 5 marks each. Covers entire syllabus breadth.
2. **Short Notes (Q5)** - 4×5=20 marks appears in 7/10 papers. Prepare 15-20 short note topics.
3. **Diagrams are crucial** - Thread Life Cycle, JDBC Architecture (2/3 tier), Inheritance Hierarchy, AWT Hierarchy.
4. **Code + Theory** - Most 10-mark questions need BOTH explanation AND program.
5. **Comparison Questions** - Very common (5-10 marks): Abstract vs Interface, Overloading vs Overriding, Checked vs Unchecked, etc.

### Topic Weightage by Marks:
- **10 Marks**: Inheritance, Polymorphism, Exception Handling, Multithreading, JDBC, Abstract Class/Interface, Collections, Streams
- **5-8 Marks**: Encapsulation, Constructor, super/final/this, Thread Life Cycle, Package, File, Serialization, JavaFX, AWT/Swing
- **5 Marks (Short)**: All differentiation questions, short programs, specific method explanations

### Study Priority Order:
1. **Week 1**: Units 5, 6, 7 (Inheritance, Polymorphism, Exceptions) - Core OOP
2. **Week 2**: Units 9, 15, 16 (Multithreading, JDBC) - Heavy weightage
3. **Week 3**: Units 4, 8, 11, 12 (Class/Object, Package, I/O, Collections) - Medium
4. **Week 4**: Units 13, 14, 2, 3, 10, 1 (GUI, Basics, Decision/Loop, String/Number) - Remaining
