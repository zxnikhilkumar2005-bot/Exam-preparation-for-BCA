## 12.1 DEFAULT AND REPOSSESSION

### **Default:**
Failure of hire purchaser to pay instalment on due date.

### **Repossession:**
Vendor takes back goods on default. Two types:

### **1. Complete Repossession (Full Default)**
- Vendor takes back **all goods**
- Hire purchaser's liability = Amount due (Opening balance + Interest - Instalments paid)
- **Loss on Repossession** = Book Value of Asset - Agreed Repossession Value

**Entries in Hire Purchaser's Books:**
```
1. On Default (Amount Due):
   Vendor A/c          Dr.    (Amount Due: Op Bal + Int - Paid)
       To Asset A/c               (Book Value)
       To P&L A/c                 (Loss on Repossession) [if any]

2. If Partial Payment After Default:
   Asset A/c             Dr.    (Agreed Value)
       To Vendor A/c
```

**Entries in Vendor's Books:**
```
1. On Repossession:
   Goods Repossessed A/c  Dr.    (Agreed Value)
       To Hire Purchaser A/c

2. On Sale of Repossessed Goods:
   Bank A/c               Dr.    (Sale Proceeds)
       To Goods Repossessed A/c
   [Profit/Loss on resale to P&L]
```

### **2. Partial Repossession (Part Default)**
- Vendor takes back **part of goods**
- Remaining goods continue on HP
- Apportion values based on cash price ratio

## 12.2 INSTALMENT PAYMENT SYSTEM vs HIRE PURCHASE 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (2/10 papers)

| Basis | **Hire Purchase System** | **Instalment Payment System** |
|-------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Ownership** | Passes on **last instalment** | Passes **immediately** on agreement |
| **Possession** | Delivered immediately | Delivered immediately |
| **Right to Repossess** | **Yes** (if default) | **No** (only sue for payment) |
| **Default Consequence** | Repossession, no refund | Sue for unpaid price |
| **Buyer's Status** | **Hirer** (bailee) | **Owner** (debtor) |
| **Risk of Loss** | On vendor (until last payment) | On buyer (immediately) |
| **Depreciation** | Charged by hirer | Charged by buyer |
| **Interest Allocation** | On reducing balance | On reducing balance |
| **Accounting (Buyer)** | Asset at Cash Price, Vendor A/c | Asset at Cash Price, Vendor A/c (similar) |
| **Accounting (Vendor)** | HP Sales + Interest Suspense | Credit Sales + Interest Income |

### **Key Difference:**
> **Hire Purchase = Conditional Sale (Ownership conditional)**
> **Instalment System = Credit Sale (Ownership immediate)**

## 12.3 GOODS OF SMALL VALUE - HIRE PURCHASE TRADING ACCOUNT 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (2/10 papers)

When goods of **small value** (e.g., cycles, sewing machines, radios) sold on HP in **large quantities**, maintaining individual accounts is impractical. Special systems used:

### **System 1: Stock and Debtors System (Invoice Price Method)**

**Accounts Maintained:**
1. **HP Stock Account** (at Invoice Price)
2. **HP Debtors Account** (at Invoice Price)
3. **HP Adjustment Account** (Loading Account)
4. **HP Trading Account** (Profit Ascertainment)

### **System 2: Hire Purchase Trading Account Method (Cost Price Method)**

**Only ONE Account:** HP Trading Account (at Cost)

## 12.4 STOCK AND DEBTORS SYSTEM (INVOICE PRICE) 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

### **Loading Calculation:**
```
If Cash Price + 25% = Invoice Price → Loading = 20% of Invoice Price
If Cash Price + 33⅓% = Invoice Price → Loading = 25% of Invoice Price
Formula: Loading % = Markup % / (100 + Markup %) × 100
```

## 12.6 COMPARISON: TWO SYSTEMS FOR SMALL VALUE GOODS

| Basis | **Stock & Debtors System** | **HP Trading Account Method** |
|-------|----------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Accounts** | 4 Accounts (Stock, Debtors, Adj, Trading) | 1 Account (Trading only) |
| **Valuation** | Invoice Price (with Loading) | Cost Price |
| **Profit Ascertainment** | Through HP Adjustment Account | Directly in HP Trading Account |
| **Suitability** | Large number of transactions | Few transactions, simple |
| **Complexity** | More detailed | Simple |
| **Stock Reserve** | Maintained (Opening/Closing) | Not maintained |

## 12.9 KEY POINTS FOR REVISION

- ✅ **Default** = Failure to pay instalment
- ✅ **Complete Repossession** = All goods taken back, Loss = Book Value - Agreed Value
- ✅ **Partial Repossession** = Part goods taken, apportion by cash price
- ✅ **Instalment System** = Ownership immediate, no repossession right
- ✅ **Small Value Goods** = Two systems: Stock & Debtors (IP) vs HP Trading (Cost)
- ✅ **Loading %** = Markup/(100+Markup)×100 (e.g., 25% markup = 20% loading)
- ✅ **Stock & Debtors System** = 4 accounts: HP Stock, HP Debtors, HP Adjustment, HP Trading
- ✅ **HP Trading Account Method** = Single account at Cost Price
- ✅ **HP Adjustment Account** = Loading account, shows gross profit from loading

## 12.10 MEMORY AIDS

### **Repossession: "BOOK VALUE - AGREED VALUE = LOSS"**
- Vendor takes goods at agreed value
- Difference = Loss to hire purchaser

### **Two Systems: "4 vs 1"**
- **Stock & Debtors** = **4** Accounts (Stock, Debtors, Adj, Trading)
- **HP Trading** = **1** Account (Trading only)

### **Loading Formula: "MARKUP DIVIDED BY 100+MARKUP"**
- 25% markup → 25/125 = 20%
- 33⅓% markup → 33.33/133.33 = 25%
- 50% markup → 50/150 = 33⅓%
