## 16.1 INVOICE PRICE METHOD

### **Concept:**
- Goods invoiced to Consignee at **Invoice Price** (Cost + Profit Margin)
- **Loading** = Invoice Price - Cost Price
- Consignment Account shows **Loading adjustments** to arrive at actual profit

### **Loading Calculations:**
| Markup on Cost | Loading on IP |
|----------------|---------------|
| Cost + 20% = IP | 16.67% |
| Cost + 25% = IP | **20%** |
| Cost + 33⅓% = IP | **25%** |
| Cost + 50% = IP | 33.33% |

**Formula:** `Loading % = Markup % / (100 + Markup %) × 100`

## 16.6 NORMAL LOSS 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (3/10 papers)

### **Definition:**
Loss inherent in nature of goods - **unavoidable, expected** (evaporation, shrinkage, leakage, drying, breakage in transit).

### **Characteristics:**
- **Expected** and **unavoidable**
- **No separate accounting entry**
- **Absorbed in cost** of good units
- **Increases unit cost** of remaining stock

### **Stock Valuation with Normal Loss:**
```
Cost of Good Units = (Total Cost - Sale Proceeds of Normal Loss Scrap) / Good Units Quantity
```

### **Example:**
1,000 kg sent @ ₹100/kg = ₹1,00,000. Normal loss 50 kg. Scrap sold ₹500.
Good units = 950 kg.
Cost per kg = (1,00,000 - 500) / 950 = ₹104.74/kg
Closing stock (100 kg) = 100 × 104.74 = ₹10,474

---

## 16.7 ABNORMAL LOSS 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (3/10 papers)

### **Definition:**
Loss due to **unexpected, avoidable** causes - accident, fire, theft, carelessness, pilferage.

### **Characteristics:**
- **Unexpected** and **avoidable**
- **Separate accounting entry** required
- **Charged to P&L** (not absorbed in stock)
- **Insurance claim** possible

### **Journal Entries:**

| Situation | Journal Entry |
|-----------|---------------|
| **1. Abnormal Loss Occurs** | Abnormal Loss A/c Dr. (Value at Cost/IP)<br>To Consignment A/c |
| **2. Insurance Claim Admitted** | Insurance Co./Bank A/c Dr. (Claim Admitted)<br>To Abnormal Loss A/c |
| **3. Net Loss Transferred to P&L** | P&L A/c Dr. (Abnormal Loss - Claim)<br>To Abnormal Loss A/c |
| **4. If No Insurance** | P&L A/c Dr. (Full Abnormal Loss)<br>To Abnormal Loss A/c |

### **Valuation of Abnormal Loss:**
- **At Cost Price Method:** Same as good units (after normal loss adjustment)
- **At Invoice Price Method:** At Invoice Price, then remove Loading via Adjustment Entry

## 16.8 NORMAL & ABNORMAL LOSS SIMULTANEOUSLY 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

### **Step-by-Step Treatment:**

1. **First:** Deduct **Normal Loss** from total quantity
2. **Second:** Calculate **Cost per Good Unit** = (Total Cost - Scrap of Normal Loss) / Good Units
3. **Third:** Value **Abnormal Loss** at this **adjusted cost per unit**
4. **Fourth:** Value **Closing Stock** at same adjusted cost per unit
5. **Fifth:** Insurance Claim reduces Abnormal Loss charged to P&L

### **Example:**
1,000 kg sent @ ₹100 = ₹1,00,000. Freight ₹10,000. Total ₹1,10,000.
Normal Loss: 50 kg. Abnormal Loss: 30 kg (fire). Scrap of normal loss: ₹500.
Insurance Claim Admitted: ₹2,500.

**Calculation:**
- Good Units = 1,000 - 50 = 950 kg
- Cost per Good Unit = (1,10,000 - 500) / 950 = ₹115.26/kg
- Abnormal Loss (30 kg) = 30 × 115.26 = ₹3,458
- Insurance Claim = ₹2,500
- Net Loss to P&L = ₹3,458 - ₹2,500 = ₹958
- Closing Stock (100 kg) = 100 × 115.26 = ₹11,526

## 16.9 DEL-CREDERE COMMISSION 🔴 MEDIUM PRIORITY (1/10 papers)

### **Definition:**
**Extra commission** paid to Consignee for **guaranteeing collection** from credit customers. Consignee bears **bad debt risk**.

### **Features:**
- Additional to ordinary commission
- Consignee **bears bad debts** on credit sales
- No bad debt entry in Consignor's books
- Higher commission rate (e.g., 5% ordinary + 2% del-credere = 7% total)

### **Accounting:**
| Transaction | Journal Entry |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Del-Credere Commission** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **Bad Debts (With Del-Credere)** | **NO ENTRY** (Consignee bears) |
| **Bad Debts (Without Del-Credere)** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |

---

## 16.10 OVER-RIDING COMMISSION

### **Definition:**
Extra commission to Consignee for **achieving sales target** or **promoting new product**.

### **Accounting:**
- Same as Ordinary Commission
- Consignment A/c Dr. / To Consignee A/c

- **G**oods Sent
- **O**pening Stock
- **C**losing Stock
- **R**eturns
- **A**bnormal Loss

### **5 Loading Entries: "GO CRA"**
- **G**oods Sent: Consignment Dr / To Goods Sent
- **O**pening Stock: Stock Reserve Dr / To Consignment
- **C**losing Stock: Consignment Dr / To Stock Reserve
- **R**eturns: Goods Sent Dr / To Consignment

### **Normal vs Abnormal: "EXPECTED vs UNEXPECTED"**
- **Normal** = Expected, Absorbed in cost
- **Abnormal** = Unexpected, Charged to P&L

### **Del-Credere: "EXTRA COMMISSION = NO BAD DEBT ENTRY"**
- Consignee guarantees collection
- Bad debts = Consignee's problem
