## 10.1 INTRODUCTION

Unit 9 covered basic adjustments. This unit covers **advanced adjustments** that frequently appear in exam problems. These adjustments ensure financial statements reflect true profit and financial position by following the **Accrual** and **Matching** concepts.

## 10.2 ADVANCED ADJUSTMENTS (MEMORIZE ALL 14)

### **Adjustment 1: Closing Stock (Recap)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Stock on 31st March valued at ₹X" | 1. **Trading A/c (Cr. side)** - Add to Closing Stock<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Show as Current Asset |

### **Adjustment 2: Outstanding Expenses (Expenses Due but Not Paid)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Salaries outstanding ₹5,000" or "Salaries due but not paid" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Add to respective expense<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Show as Current Liability |

**Entry:** `Expense A/c Dr. / To Outstanding Expense A/c`

### **Adjustment 3: Prepaid Expenses (Expenses Paid in Advance)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Insurance prepaid ₹2,000" or "Insurance paid in advance" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Deduct from respective expense<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Show as Current Asset |

**Entry:** `Prepaid Expense A/c Dr. / To Expense A/c`

### **Adjustment 4: Accrued Income (Income Earned but Not Received)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Commission accrued ₹3,000" or "Interest earned but not received" | 1. **P&L A/c (Cr. side)** - Add to respective income<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Show as Current Asset |

**Entry:** `Accrued Income A/c Dr. / To Income A/c`

### **Adjustment 5: Income Received in Advance (Unearned Income)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Rent received in advance ₹4,000" | 1. **P&L A/c (Cr. side)** - Deduct from respective income<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Show as Current Liability |

**Entry:** `Income A/c Dr. / To Income Received in Advance A/c`

### **Adjustment 6: Depreciation on Fixed Assets**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Depreciate Machinery @10% p.a." or "Provide depreciation on Furniture @15%" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Show as Depreciation Expense<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Deduct from respective asset |

**Entry:** `Depreciation A/c Dr. / To Asset A/c` (or Provision for Depreciation A/c)

**Calculation:**
- **SLM:** (Cost - Scrap) / Life × Months/12
- **WDV:** Rate × WDV at beginning × Months/12
- **If addition during year:** Depreciate for period used

### **Adjustment 7: Bad Debts (Irrecoverable Debts)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Bad debts ₹2,000" or "Write off ₹2,000 as bad debts" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Add to Bad Debts Expense<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Deduct from Debtors |

**Entry:** `Bad Debts A/c Dr. / To Debtors A/c`

### **Adjustment 8: Provision for Bad Debts (Doubtful Debts)**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Create provision for bad debts @5% on Debtors" or "Maintain provision at ₹X" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - **New Provision - Old Provision** (if new > old, expense; if new < old, income)<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Asset side)** - Deduct New Provision from Debtors |

**Entry (New > Old):** `P&L A/c Dr. / To Provision for Bad Debts A/c`
**Entry (New < Old):** `Provision for Bad Debts A/c Dr. / To P&L A/c`

**Calculation:**
```
New Provision = (Debtors - Bad Debts) × Rate%
P&L Charge = New Provision - Old Provision (given in TB)
```

### **Adjustment 9: Interest on Capital**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Allow interest on capital @8% p.a." | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Show as Interest on Capital<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Add to Capital (if not paid) OR show as Current Liability |

**Entry:** `Interest on Capital A/c Dr. / To Capital A/c` (or To Interest Payable A/c)

**Calculation:** `Opening Capital × Rate × Months/12` (if additional capital introduced, calculate proportionately)

### **Adjustment 10: Interest on Drawings**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Charge interest on drawings @6% p.a." | 1. **P&L A/c (Cr. side)** - Show as Interest on Drawings (Income)<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Deduct from Capital (if not paid) OR show as Current Asset |

**Entry:** `Drawings A/c Dr. / To Interest on Drawings A/c` (or Capital A/c Dr.)

**Calculation Methods:**
- **Fixed amount throughout year:** `Total Drawings × Rate × 6/12` (average 6 months)
- **If dates given:** Calculate per drawing: `Amount × Rate × Months outstanding/12`
- **If not specified:** Assume 6 months average

### **Adjustment 11: Commission on Profits**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Manager's commission @10% on net profit" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Show as Commission Expense<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Show as Outstanding Commission |

**Two Bases of Calculation:**

| Basis | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| **On Profit BEFORE Commission** | `Profit before Commission × Rate%` |
| **On Profit AFTER Commission** | `Profit before Commission × Rate% / (100 + Rate%)` |

**Example:** Profit before commission ₹1,00,000, Commission 10%
- Before: ₹1,00,000 × 10% = ₹10,000
- After: ₹1,00,000 × 10/110 = ₹9,091

### **Adjustment 12: Goods Distributed as Free Samples / Advertising**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Goods costing ₹5,000 distributed as free samples" | 1. **P&L A/c (Dr. side)** - Add to Advertising/Selling Expenses<br>2. **Trading A/c (Cr. side)** - Deduct from Purchases (or show separately) |

**Entry:** `Advertising A/c Dr. / To Purchases A/c` (at cost)

### **Adjustment 13: Goods Taken by Proprietor for Personal Use**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Proprietor took goods worth ₹3,000 for personal use" | 1. **Trading A/c (Cr. side)** - Deduct from Purchases (at cost)<br>2. **Balance Sheet (Liability side)** - Deduct from Capital (as Drawings) |

**Entry:** `Drawings A/c Dr. / To Purchases A/c` (at cost)

### **Adjustment 14: Sale of Goods on Approval / Sale or Return Basis**
| Given | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| "Goods sent on approval ₹10,000 (cost ₹7,000), not yet approved" | 1. **Reverse the sale:** Deduct from Sales, Add to Stock at cost<br>2. **Balance Sheet:** Show stock at cost with customers (separate note) |

**Entry (when sent):** `Customer A/c Dr. / To Sales A/c` (at sale price)
**Entry (if not approved at year-end):** `Sales A/c Dr. / To Customer A/c` (reverse sale)
**Stock adjustment:** `Stock with Customers A/c Dr. / To Trading A/c` (at cost)

## 10.4 STEP-BY-STEP APPROACH TO FINAL ACCOUNTS PROBLEMS

### **Step 1: Prepare Trial Balance**
- List all accounts with Dr./Cr. balances
- Verify Totals match

### **Step 2: Process Each Adjustment**
For EACH adjustment:
1. Identify accounts affected
2. Determine Dr./Cr. in P&L/Trading
3. Determine Balance Sheet effect
4. Pass Journal Entry (mental or written)

### **Step 3: Prepare Trading Account**
- Opening Stock, Purchases (adjusted), Direct Expenses
- Sales (adjusted), Closing Stock
- Calculate Gross Profit/Loss

### **Step 4: Prepare Profit & Loss Account**
- Gross Profit b/d
- All Indirect Expenses (adjusted)
- All Incomes (adjusted)
- Calculate Net Profit/Loss

### **Step 5: Prepare Balance Sheet**
- Capital (Opening + Net Profit - Drawings - Interest on Drawings + Interest on Capital)
- Liabilities (Loans, Creditors, Outstanding Exp, Advance Income, Provisions, O/s Commission)
- Assets (Fixed Assets - Dep, Stock, Debtors - Bad Debts - Provision, Cash, Bank, Prepaid, Accrued, Stock with Customers)

## 10.5 COST OF GOODS SOLD CALCULATION

### **Formula:**
```
Cost of Goods Sold = Opening Stock + Net Purchases + Direct Expenses - Closing Stock
```

### **Where:**
- **Net Purchases** = Purchases - Purchase Returns - Goods as Samples - Drawings Goods - Goods on Approval (at cost)
- **Direct Expenses** = Wages, Carriage Inwards, Freight, Factory Rent, Power, etc.

## 10.6 HIDDEN ADJUSTMENTS (OFTEN MISSED)

| Hidden in Problem | Actual Adjustment Needed |
|-------------------|--------------------------|
| "Wages include ₹5,000 for installation of machine" | Capitalize ₹5,000 to Machine, reduce Wages |
| "Advertising includes ₹10,000 for permanent signboard" | Capitalize to Asset, reduce Advertising |
| "Purchases include ₹20,000 for furniture" | Capitalize to Furniture, reduce Purchases |
| "Sales include ₹15,000 for old furniture sold" | Remove from Sales, credit Furniture/Asset Disposal |
| "Rent paid for 14 months" | Prepaid for 2 months |
| "Insurance policy for 2 years paid" | Prepaid for next year |
| "Bad debts recovered during year" | Credit to Bad Debts Recovered (P&L Cr.) |

## 10.8 KEY POINTS FOR REVISION

- ✅ **14 Standard Adjustments** - Memorize treatment for each (P&L/Trading + Balance Sheet + Entry)
- ✅ **Provision for Bad Debts** = On (Debtors - Bad Debts) × Rate%; P&L charge = New - Old
- ✅ **Interest on Capital** = Opening Capital × Rate × Months/12
- ✅ **Interest on Drawings** = Total Drawings × Rate × 6/12 (avg) or per date
- ✅ **Commission on Profit:** Before = Profit × Rate%; After = Profit × Rate/(100+Rate)
- ✅ **Goods Samples/Drawings** = At COST (not sale price), reduce Purchases
- ✅ **Sale on Approval** = Reverse sale at selling price, add stock at cost
- ✅ **Hidden Adjustments** - Capitalize revenue expenses for assets
- ✅ **Order in P&L:** Gross Profit → Incomes → Expenses → Net Profit

## 10.9 MEMORY AIDS

### **5 Basic Adjustments: "OSPAA"**
- **O**utstanding Expenses
- **S**tock (Closing)
- **P**repaid Expenses
- **A**ccrued Income
- **A**dvance Income

### **5 Advanced Adjustments: "DPBCI"**
- **D**epreciation
- **P**rovision for Bad Debts
- **B**ad Debts
- **C**ommission on Profit
- **I**nterest on Capital/Drawings

### **Commission Formula: "BEFORE = Simple, AFTER = Divide by 100+Rate"**
- **Before:** Profit × Rate%
- **After:** Profit × Rate/(100+Rate%)
