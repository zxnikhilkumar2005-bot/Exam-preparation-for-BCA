## 9.1 INTRODUCTION

**Final Accounts** are the **summary statements** prepared at the end of an accounting period to ascertain:
1. **Profit or Loss** → Trading & Profit and Loss Account
2. **Financial Position** → Balance Sheet

These are called **Final Accounts** because they are the final output of the accounting process.

## 9.2 TRADING ACCOUNT

### **Purpose:**
To ascertain **Gross Profit** or **Gross Loss** from buying and selling of goods.

### **Formula:**
> **Gross Profit = Net Sales - Cost of Goods Sold**

### **Components:**

| Dr. Side (Debits) | Cr. Side (Credits) |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Opening Stock | Sales |
| Purchases | Less: Sales Returns |
| Less: Purchase Returns | Closing Stock |
| Direct Expenses | |
| - Wages | |
| - Carriage Inwards | |
| - Freight/Dock Charges | |
| - Import Duty | |
| - Factory Rent/Power | |
| **Gross Profit c/d** (Balancing) | **Gross Loss c/d** (Balancing) |

### **Format:**


## 9.3 PROFIT AND LOSS ACCOUNT

### **Purpose:**
To ascertain **Net Profit** or **Net Loss** after all indirect expenses and incomes.

### **Formula:**
> **Net Profit = Gross Profit + Other Incomes - Indirect Expenses**

### **Components:**

## 9.5 MANUFACTURING ACCOUNT 🔴 MEDIUM PRIORITY (2/10 papers - 3+7 marks)

To ascertain **Cost of Production** for manufacturing firms (who convert raw materials into finished goods).

## 9.6 ADJUSTMENTS IN FINAL ACCOUNTS (BASIC)

### **Need for Adjustments:**
To follow **Accrual Concept** and **Matching Concept** - recognize revenues earned and expenses incurred in the period, regardless of cash flow.

### **Common Adjustments:**

| Adjustment | Treatment in Final Accounts |
|------------|----------------------------|
| **1. Closing Stock** | Dr. Trading A/c, Cr. Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **2. Outstanding Expenses** | Dr. P&L (add to expense), Cr. Balance Sheet (Liability) |
| **3. Prepaid Expenses** | Dr. P&L (deduct from expense), Dr. Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **4. Accrued Income** | Dr. P&L (add to income), Dr. Balance Sheet (Asset) |
| **5. Income Received in Advance** | Dr. P&L (deduct from income), Cr. Balance Sheet (Liability) |

## 9.7 MARSHALLING OF BALANCE SHEET

### **Order of Permanence (Traditional):**
- **Liabilities:** Capital → Long-term Loans → Current Liabilities
- **Assets:** Fixed Assets (most permanent) → Current Assets (least permanent)

### **Order of Liquidity (Modern):**
- **Liabilities:** Current Liabilities → Long-term Loans → Capital
- **Assets:** Cash → Debtors → Stock → Fixed Assets

## 9.9 KEY POINTS FOR REVISION

- ✅ **Final Accounts** = Trading A/c + P&L A/c + Balance Sheet
- ✅ **Trading A/c** = Gross Profit (Sales - COGS)
- ✅ **P&L A/c** = Net Profit (Gross Profit + Incomes - Indirect Expenses)
- ✅ **Balance Sheet** = Financial Position (Assets = Liabilities + Capital)
- ✅ **Manufacturing A/c** = Cost of Production → transferred to Trading A/c
- ✅ **Direct Expenses** = Wages, Carriage Inwards, Freight, Factory Rent, Power
- ✅ **Indirect Expenses** = Salaries, Rent, Office Expenses, Depreciation, etc.
- ✅ **Basic 5 Adjustments:** Closing Stock, Outstanding, Prepaid, Accrued Income, Advance Income
- ✅ **Marshalling:** Order of Permanence (Fixed→Current) or Liquidity (Current→Fixed)
