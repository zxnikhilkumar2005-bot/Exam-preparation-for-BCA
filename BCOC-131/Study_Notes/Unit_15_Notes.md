# UNIT 15: CONSIGNMENT ACCOUNTS-I

**Block 5: Consignments and Joint Ventures** | **PYQ Priority: 🔴 HIGH** (9/10 papers - Consignment Account appears in 90% of papers)

---

## 15.1 INTRODUCTION

**Consignment** is a business arrangement where:
- **Consignor** (Owner/Principal) sends goods to **Consignee** (Agent)
- Consignee sells goods **on behalf of Consignor**
- Consignee earns **commission** on sales
- **Ownership** remains with Consignor until sold
- **Risk** remains with Consignor

---

## 15.2 CONCEPTS OF CONSIGNMENT

### **Parties:**
| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Consignor** | Owner of goods, sends goods, bears risk, receives proceeds |
| **Consignee** | Agent, sells goods, earns commission, remits balance |

### **Key Terms:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Proforma Invoice** | Statement sent with goods showing quantity, price, terms (not a real invoice) |
| **Account Sales** | Periodic statement from Consignee showing sales, expenses, commission, balance due |
| **Commission** | Remuneration to Consignee (Ordinary, Del-Credere, Over-riding) |
| **Advance** | Payment made by Consignee to Consignor before sales |
| **Loading** | Difference between Invoice Price and Cost Price |

### **Sale vs Consignment:**

| Basis | **Sale** | **Consignment** |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Ownership** | Transfers immediately | Remains with Consignor |
| **Risk** | Transfers to buyer | Remains with Consignor |
| **Relationship** | Seller-Buyer | Principal-Agent |
| **Return of Goods** | Not allowed (unless defect) | Allowed (unsold goods) |
| **Profit/Loss** | Seller's | Consignor's |
| **Expenses** | Buyer's after sale | Consignor's (mostly) |
| **Account Sales** | Not required | Required periodically |

---

## 15.3 ACCOUNTING TREATMENT

### **Books of Consignor:**

**Accounts Maintained:**
1. **Consignment Account** (Nominal - Profit/Loss)
2. **Consignee Account** (Personal - Receivable)
3. **Goods Sent on Consignment Account** (Real - at Cost or IP)
4. **Consignment Stock Account** (Real - Unsold stock)

### **Books of Consignee:**

**Accounts Maintained:**
1. **Consignor Account** (Personal - Payable)
2. **Commission Account** (Income)
3. **Expenses Account** (If paid on behalf)
4. **Stock Account** (Optional - for own record)

---

## 15.4 JOURNAL ENTRIES IN BOOKS OF CONSIGNOR

### **At Cost Price Method:**

| Transaction | Journal Entry |
|-------------|---------------|
| **1. Goods Sent on Consignment** | Consignment A/c Dr. (Cost)<br>To Goods Sent on Consignment A/c (Cost) |
| **2. Consignor's Expenses (Freight, Insurance, Loading)** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Cash/Bank A/c |
| **3. Advance from Consignee** | Cash/Bank A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **4. Bills Receivable from Consignee** | Bills Receivable A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **5. Sales by Consignee (Intimated via Account Sales)** | Consignee A/c Dr. (Sale Value)<br>To Consignment A/c (Sale Value) |
| **6. Consignee's Expenses** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **7. Commission (Ordinary)** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **8. Del-Credere Commission** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **9. Bad Debts (if no Del-Credere)** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |
| **10. Bad Debts (if Del-Credere given)** | **No Entry** (Consignee bears) |
| **11. Unsold Stock Valuation** | Consignment Stock A/c Dr.<br>To Consignment A/c |
| **12. Profit on Consignment** | Consignment A/c Dr.<br>To P&L A/c |
| **13. Loss on Consignment** | P&L A/c Dr.<br>To Consignment A/c |
| **14. Settlement from Consignee** | Cash/Bank/Bills Receivable Dr.<br>To Consignee A/c |

## 15.7 UNSOLD STOCK VALUATION 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY

### **At Cost Price Method:**

```
Cost of Unsold Stock = (Cost of Goods Sent × Unsold Quantity) / Total Quantity Sent
                    + Proportionate Non-recurring Expenses of Consignor
                    (Freight, Insurance, Loading charges - not recurring expenses)
```

### **Formula:**
```
Stock Value = (Cost of Goods Sent + Consignor's Non-recurring Expenses) 
              × Unsold Qty / Total Qty Sent
```

### **Non-recurring Expenses (Included in Stock Value):**
- Freight/Carriage to Consignee
- Loading/Unloading charges
- Insurance in transit
- Dock charges
- Import duty

### **Recurring Expenses (NOT Included in Stock Value):**
- Godown rent
- Selling expenses
- Advertising
- Salary of salesman
- Consignee's expenses

---

## 15.8 DIRECT RECORDING IN LEDGER (ALTERNATIVE METHOD)

Instead of Journal → Ledger, entries recorded directly in Ledger Accounts.

### **Consignment Account (Direct):**
- **Dr. Side:** Goods Sent, Expenses, Commission, Bad Debts
- **Cr. Side:** Sales, Returns, Abnormal Loss, Stock, Profit

### **Consignee Account (Direct):**
- **Dr. Side:** Sales
- **Cr. Side:** Advance, Expenses, Commission, Bad Debts, Settlement

## 15.10 KEY POINTS FOR REVISION

- ✅ **Consignment** = Principal-Agent, Ownership with Consignor, Risk with Consignor
- ✅ **Proforma Invoice** = Not a real invoice, just information
- ✅ **Account Sales** = Statement from Consignee (Sales, Expenses, Commission, Balance)
- ✅ **Consignment A/c** = Nominal A/c (Profit/Loss), **Consignee A/c** = Personal A/c
- ✅ **Stock Valuation** = Cost + Proportionate Non-recurring Expenses
- ✅ **Non-recurring** = Freight, Insurance, Loading (included in stock)
- ✅ **Recurring** = Godown rent, Selling expenses (NOT in stock)
- ✅ **Del-Credere Commission** = Extra commission for bearing bad debt risk
- ✅ **Bad Debts** = Consignor bears (unless Del-Credere given)

## 15.11 MEMORY AIDS

### **Stock Valuation: "COST + NON-RECURRING"**
- **Include:** Freight, Insurance, Loading, Dock charges
- **Exclude:** Godown rent, Selling exp, Salary, Advertising

### **Consignment Accounts: "C-C-G-S"**
- **C**onsignment A/c (Profit/Loss)
- **C**onsignee A/c (Personal)
- **G**oods Sent on Consignment A/c
- **S**tock A/c (Unsold)

### **Expenses: "CONSIGNOR PAYS, CONSIGNEE REIMBURSES"**
- Consignor's expenses → Consignment A/c Dr
- Consignee's expenses → Consignment A/c Dr / To Consignee
