# BCOC-131 FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING - IMPORTANT QUESTIONS BANK

**Based on PYQ Analysis (10 Years: Dec 2021 - June 2026)**

---

## 📋 QUESTION CATEGORISATION BY MARKS

### **2 MARKS QUESTIONS (Short Answer / Definition)**
- Definitions, Concepts, Differences, Formulae
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes each
- **Format:** 2-3 sentences or bullet points

### **5 MARKS QUESTIONS (Short Notes / Explanation)**
- Concepts with examples, Distinctions, Procedures
- **Time:** 8-10 minutes each
- **Format:** 150-200 words, structured answer

### **10 MARKS QUESTIONS (Detailed Theory)**
- Comprehensive explanations, Multiple sub-parts
- **Time:** 18-20 minutes each
- **Format:** 300-400 words, introduction-body-conclusion

### **15+5 / 20 MARKS QUESTIONS (Practical Problems)**
- Full accounting problems with ledger accounts
- **Time:** 30-36 minutes each
- **Format:** Proper ledger format, working notes, narrations
