## 17.1 INTRODUCTION

**Joint Venture (JV)** is a **temporary partnership** between two or more persons for a **specific project/business venture**. It ends when the venture is completed.

### **Key Features:**
| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Specific Purpose** | Single project (construction, underwriting, trading) |
| **No Firm Name** | Operates under co-venturers' names |
| **Temporary** | Dissolves on completion |
| **Profit Sharing** | Agreed ratio (not necessarily capital ratio) |
| **No Separate Legal Entity** | Co-venturers personally liable |
| **Co-venturers** = Partners in JV |

### **JV vs Consignment vs Partnership:**

| Basis | **Joint Venture** | **Consignment** | **Partnership** |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Relationship** | Co-venturers (partners) | Principal-Agent | Partners |
| **Duration** | Specific venture | Ongoing | Continuous |
| **Ownership** | Joint | Consignor | Joint |
| **Profit Sharing** | Agreed ratio | Commission only | Profit sharing ratio |
| **Accounting** | Various methods | Consignment A/c | Partnership books |
| **Acts** | Each can bind | Agent binds principal | Each partner binds firm |

## 17.2 METHODS OF ACCOUNTING FOR JOINT VENTURE 🔴 HIGHEST PRIORITY

### **Method 1: Separate Set of Books (Rare in Exams)**
- Separate books maintained for JV
- Joint Bank A/c, Joint Venture A/c, Co-venturers' A/cs
- All transactions through Joint Bank

### **Method 2: In Own Books Only (Memorandum JV Account) - MOST COMMON IN EXAMS 🔴**
- Each co-venturer records **only own transactions**
- Prepares **Memorandum JV Account** for profit calculation
- **Co-venturer's Account** maintained in own books
- Settlement via Bank Draft

### **Method 3: In Books of One Co-venturer**
- One co-venturer maintains all JV records
- Other co-venturers only record settlement

## 17.6 UNDERWRITING / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION JOINT VENTURE 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (June 2024, June 2026)

### **Special Features:**
- **Venture:** Underwrite shares of a company
- **Income:** Underwriting Commission + Premium on shares sold
- **Loss:** Shares taken up at cost - Market value
- **Profit Sharing:** Agreed ratio

### **Typical Problem Data:**
- Underwriting agreement (e.g., 50,000 shares @ ₹10, premium ₹2)
- Commission rate (e.g., 4%)
- Applications received (e.g., 38,000 shares)
- Balance taken up by underwriters
- Shares sold in market / taken up
- Settlement

### **Accounts to Prepare:**
1. **Memorandum JV Account**
2. **Joint Venture with Other Co-venturer Account** (in each co-venturer's books)

## 17.7 CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT JOINT VENTURE 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (June 2026)

### **Special Features:**
- **Contract Price** fixed
- **Materials, Labour, Plant** purchased
- **Progress Payments** received
- **Plant** may be taken over at end
- **Profit** = Contract Price - Total Cost

### **Typical Transactions:**
- A purchases materials, pays expenses
- B takes loan, purchases materials
- A remits cash to B
- B pays expenses, draws bill on A
- Bill discounted
- Loan repaid with interest
- Contract completed, final payments received
- Plant taken over by one co-venturer

## 17.8 STEP-BY-STEP PROBLEM SOLVING (METHOD 2 - MEMORANDUM JV) 🔴 HIGHEST PRIORITY

### **Given Data Usually Includes:**
- Co-venturers A & B, Profit sharing ratio (e.g., 2:1 or 3:2)
- Purchases by A, Purchases by B
- Expenses by A, Expenses by B
- Sales by A, Sales by B
- Unsold stock taken over by A/B at agreed value
- Advances/Remittances between co-venturers
- Bills of exchange drawn/accepted/discounted
- Final settlement

### **Steps:**

1. **Prepare Memorandum JV Account** (Combined - all transactions)
   - Dr.: All Purchases + All Expenses + Profit shares
   - Cr.: All Sales + Closing Stock taken over + Profit shares

2. **Calculate Profit** = Cr. Total - Dr. Total (before profit)
   - Distribute in profit sharing ratio

3. **Prepare "Joint Venture with B" Account in A's Books**
   - Dr.: A's Purchases, A's Expenses, A's Profit Share, Settlement Paid
   - Cr.: A's Sales, Remittances Received from B, Settlement Received

4. **Prepare "Joint Venture with A" Account in B's Books**
   - Dr.: B's Purchases, B's Expenses, B's Profit Share, Settlement Paid
   - Cr.: B's Sales, Remittances Received from A, Settlement Received

5. **Verify:** Both co-venturer accounts should close to zero after settlement

## 17.10 KEY POINTS FOR REVISION

- ✅ **Joint Venture** = Temporary partnership for specific purpose
- ✅ **3 Methods:** (1) Separate Books, (2) Memorandum JV + Co-venturer A/cs (MOST COMMON), (3) One Co-venturer's Books
- ✅ **Method 2 (Exam Standard):** Memorandum JV (combined) + Individual JV with Other Co-venturer A/c in each book
- ✅ **Profit Sharing** = Agreed ratio (not necessarily capital ratio)
- ✅ **Unsold Stock** = Taken over at agreed value, credited to Memorandum JV
- ✅ **Settlement** = Bank draft/transfer to balance co-venturer accounts
- ✅ **Underwriting JV** = Commission + Premium income, Shares taken up = Cost
- ✅ **Construction JV** = Contract price - Total cost = Profit
