## 8.1 INTRODUCTION

**Depreciation** is the systematic allocation of the **cost of a fixed asset** over its **useful life**. It represents the **expired portion** of the asset's cost due to usage, time, obsolescence, or depletion.

---

## 8.2 DEFINITION OF DEPRECIATION

### **Definitions by Authorities:**

| Authority | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Pickles** | "Permanent and continuous diminution in the quality, quantity, or value of an asset." |
| **Spicer & Pegler** | "Measure of the exhaustion of the effective life of an asset from any cause during a given period." |
| **ICMA (London)** | "Diminution in intrinsic value of the asset due to use and/or lapse of time." |
| **Walter B. Meigs** | "Part of the cost of a fixed asset to its owner which is not recoverable when finally put out of use." |
| **ICAI** | "Measure of wearing out, consumption, or other loss of value of a depreciable asset." |

### **Simple Definition:**
> **Depreciation = Allocation of asset's cost over its useful life = Expired cost of asset**

## 8.3 DEPRECIATION vs RELATED CONCEPTS 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (2/10 papers - 2+6+12 / 7+13 marks)

### **Comparison Table:**

| Aspect | **Depreciation** | **Depletion** | **Amortization** | **Obsolescence** |
|--------|------------------|---------------|------------------|------------------|
| **Asset Type** | Tangible Fixed Assets (Machinery, Building) | Wasting Assets (Mines, Quarries, Oil Wells) | Intangible Assets (Patents, Copyrights, Goodwill) | Any Asset |
| **Cause** | Wear & tear, Time, Usage | Physical extraction/exhaustion | Time, Legal expiry, Use | Technology change, Fashion, Innovation |
| **Method** | SLM, WDV, etc. | Units of Production | Straight Line (usually) | Sudden write-down |
| **Period** | Over useful life | Over extraction period | Over legal/economic life | Sudden (when obsolete) |
| **Example** | Machinery, Building, Furniture | Coal mine, Oil well | Patent (20 yrs), Copyright | Old model machine, Outdated software |

### **Key Differences:**

| Depreciation vs Depletion | Depreciation vs Amortization |
|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| Tangible vs Wasting assets | Tangible vs Intangible assets |
| Wear & tear vs Extraction | Physical vs Legal expiry |
| SLM/WDV vs Units of Production | Various methods vs Straight Line |

| Depreciation vs Obsolescence | Obsolescence as Cause of Depreciation |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Gradual vs Sudden | Obsolescence is ONE CAUSE of depreciation |
| Wear & tear vs Technology change | Can cause accelerated depreciation |

## 8.4 CAUSES OF DEPRECIATION

1. **Wear and Tear** - Physical deterioration from usage (major cause)
2. **Lapse of Time (Efflux of Time)** - Natural deterioration over time (leases, patents)
3. **Obsolescence** - Technology changes, new models, fashion changes
4. **Depletion** - Extraction of natural resources (mines, quarries)
5. **Accidents** - Fire, flood, damage (abnormal, not regular depreciation)

---

## 8.5 OBJECTIVES OF PROVIDING DEPRECIATION

| Objective | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| **1. True Profit** | Match expired cost against revenue of the period |
| **2. True Financial Position** | Show assets at written-down value (not inflated) |
| **3. Funds for Replacement** | Retain profits to accumulate replacement funds |
| **4. Legal Compliance** | Companies Act, Income Tax Act require depreciation |
| **5. Cost of Production** | Include in product cost for pricing decisions |

---

## 8.6 FACTORS INFLUENCING DEPRECIATION

1. **Original Cost** = Purchase price + Installation + Freight + Setup costs
2. **Estimated Useful Life** - Years/units asset will be used
3. **Estimated Residual/Scrap Value** - Value at end of useful life
4. **Method of Depreciation** - SLM, WDV, etc.
5. **Legal/Statutory Requirements** - Rates prescribed by law

## 8.7 METHODS OF RECORDING DEPRECIATION

### **Method 1: Direct Charge to Asset Account (No Provision Account)**
```
Depreciation A/c    Dr.
    To Asset A/c
P&L A/c             Dr.
    To Depreciation A/c
```
**Asset shown at WDV in Balance Sheet**

### **Method 2: Provision for Depreciation / Accumulated Depreciation Account**
```
Depreciation A/c    Dr.
    To Provision for Depreciation A/c
P&L A/c             Dr.
    To Depreciation A/c
```
**Asset shown at Original Cost, Provision deducted in Balance Sheet**

> **Exam Preference:** Method 2 (Provision Account) is more common in modern practice and exams.

---

## 8.8 METHODS OF PROVIDING DEPRECIATION 🔴 HIGHEST PRIORITY

### **1. FIXED INSTALMENT METHOD (Straight Line Method - SLM)**

#### **Formula:**
```
Annual Depreciation = (Original Cost - Scrap Value) / Useful Life (years)
```

#### **Features:**
- **Constant amount** every year
- **Book value becomes zero** (or scrap value) at end
- **Simple** to calculate and understand
- **Suitable for:** Assets with uniform usage (leasehold, patents, furniture)

#### **Example:**
Cost ₹1,00,000, Scrap ₹10,000, Life 10 years
```
Depreciation = (1,00,000 - 10,000) / 10 = ₹9,000 per year
Year 1: BV = 91,000 | Year 2: BV = 82,000 | ... | Year 10: BV = 10,000
```

### **2. DIMINISHING BALANCE METHOD (Written Down Value - WDV)**

#### **Formula:**
```
Annual Depreciation = Rate × Written Down Value (at beginning of year)
```
**Rate determined by:** `Rate = 1 - n√(Scrap Value / Cost)`

#### **Features:**
- **Decreasing amount** every year
- **Book value NEVER reaches zero**
- **Higher depreciation** in early years
- **Repairs + Depreciation ≈ Constant** (as repairs increase, depreciation decreases)
- **Suitable for:** Assets with high early efficiency (machinery, vehicles, plant)

#### **Example:**
Cost ₹1,00,000, Rate 10%, Life 10 years (assume scrap ~₹38,742)
```
Year 1: Dep = 1,00,000 × 10% = 10,000 | BV = 90,000
Year 2: Dep = 90,000 × 10% = 9,000    | BV = 81,000
Year 3: Dep = 81,000 × 10% = 8,100    | BV = 72,900
...
```

---

## 8.9 COMPARISON: SLM vs WDV 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (Appears in almost every paper)

| Basis | **Fixed Instalment (SLM)** | **Diminishing Balance (WDV)** |
|-------|----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Formula** | (Cost - Scrap) / Life | Rate × WDV (beginning) |
| **Annual Amount** | **Constant** every year | **Decreases** every year |
| **Book Value at End** | Zero or Scrap Value | **Never reaches zero** |
| **Charge to P&L** | Equal each year | Higher in early years |
| **Repairs + Depreciation** | Increases over time | **Roughly constant** |
| **Suitability** | Uniform usage assets | High early efficiency assets |
| **Calculation Base** | Original Cost | Written Down Value |
| **Rate Determination** | Simple | Complex (formula needed) |
| **Income Tax (India)** | Not allowed for tax | **Allowed** (WDV method) |
| **Book Value Formula** | Cost - (Dep × Years) | Cost × (1 - Rate)^Years |

## 8.10 MACHINERY ACCOUNT & MACHINERY DISPOSAL ACCOUNT 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY (3/10 papers)

### **Standard Problem Pattern:**
- Machine purchased (maybe multiple)
- Additional purchase during period
- Sale/disposal of part/full machine
- Depreciation at X% on SLM or WDV
- Prepare Machinery A/c for 3-4 years + Disposal A/c

### **Key Journal Entries:**

```
1. PURCHASE:
   Machinery A/c           Dr.    (Cost)
       To Bank/Vendor A/c

2. ADDITIONAL PURCHASE:
   Machinery A/c           Dr.    (Cost)
       To Bank/Vendor A/c

3. DEPRECIATION (Year-end):
   Depreciation A/c        Dr.    (Amount)
       To Machinery A/c              (SLM - direct)
       OR
       To Provision for Depreciation A/c  (WDV - provision method)

4. TRANSFER TO P&L:
   Profit & Loss A/c       Dr.    (Depreciation)
       To Depreciation A/c

5. SALE/DISPOSAL:
   Bank A/c                Dr.    (Sale Proceeds)
   Provision for Dep. A/c  Dr.    (Accumulated Dep on sold asset)  [if provision method]
   Machinery Disposal A/c  Dr.    (Book Value of sold asset)
       To Machinery A/c              (Original Cost of sold asset)
       To P&L A/c                    (Profit/Loss on Disposal)

6. MACHINERY DISPOSAL ACCOUNT:
   To Machinery A/c        (Original Cost)
   To Bank A/c             (Expenses on sale)
       By Provision for Dep. A/c  (Accumulated Dep)
       By Bank A/c                (Sale Proceeds)
       By P&L A/c                 (Loss on Disposal - if any)
   [Balance = Profit on Disposal]
```

## 8.11 CHANGE OF DEPRECIATION METHOD

### **When Changed:**
- Retrospective effect (AS 6 / Ind AS 8)
- Adjustment through P&L in year of change
- Disclosure required

### **Journal Entry:**
```
Depreciation A/c    Dr.    (Difference)
    To Machinery A/c            (If SLM → WDV, additional dep)
    OR
Machinery A/c       Dr.    (Difference)
    To Depreciation A/c         (If WDV → SLM, excess dep reversed)
```

## 8.12 DEPRECIATION UNDER COMPANIES ACT / INCOME TAX ACT

| Aspect | Companies Act (Schedule II) | Income Tax Act |
|--------|----------------------------|----------------|
| **Method** | SLM or WDV (company choice) | **WDV only** (Block of Assets) |
| **Rates** | Useful life based | Prescribed rates per block |
| **Shift** | Allowed with disclosure | Not allowed (block continues) |
| **Additional Dep** | Not specified | Allowed for new assets |

## 8.15 MEMORY AIDS

### **SLM vs WDV: "CONSTANT vs DECREASING"**
- **S**LM = **S**teady (Constant)
- **W**DV = **W**aning (Decreasing)

### **Disposal Account: "TO MACHINERY, TO BANK / BY PROVISION, BY BANK, BY P&L"**
- **Dr. side:** What we gave up (Machine cost + Sale expenses)
- **Cr. side:** What we got back (Accumulated dep + Sale proceeds + P&L)

### **Methods of Recording: "DIRECT vs PROVISION"**
- **Direct:** Asset A/c credited directly (Asset at WDV)
- **Provision:** Accumulated Dep A/c credited (Asset at Cost - Provision)

## 8.16 QUICK REFERENCE FORMULAS

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| **SLM Annual Depreciation** | (Cost - Scrap Value) / Useful Life |
| **WDV Annual Depreciation** | Rate × WDV at beginning of year |
| **WDV Rate** | 1 - n√(Scrap Value / Cost) |
| **WDV after n years** | Cost × (1 - Rate)^n |
| **Profit/Loss on Disposal** | Sale Proceeds - Book Value (WDV) |
| **Book Value (SLM)** | Cost - (Annual Dep × Years) |
| **Book Value (WDV)** | Cost × (1 - Rate)^Years |
